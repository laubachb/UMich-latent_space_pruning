Latent-Space Pruning Results

Seven latent spaces (SOAP, Behler–Parrinello, bispectrum, MTP, ChIMES A-matrix, ChIMES fingerprint, and the Euler characteristic curve as a topological reference) and a random baseline each select training subsets from the same pool of DFT frames. The three figures show what each space picks (category composition), how much information the picks retain (QUESTS, judged in one descriptor-agnostic atomic space), and why (category isolation in the latent space against selection enrichment). Atom-centred descriptors (SOAP, Behler–Parrinello, bispectrum, MTP) enter as their per-atom mean, the linear design row divided by N. Choose an element and a subsampling rule.

Highlighted cells: surface share above twice its dataset share, strained share above 1.5× its dataset share, ρ ≥ 0.85, and diversity above the random baseline at 5 %. ρ is computed across the dataset's structure categories (7–9 per element).

Samplers. FPS: greedy max-min (random first point), nested cuts, extreme-seeking. k-medoids: k-means with k = cut size, one medoid per cluster, density-representative. Leverage: frames drawn without replacement with probability proportional to their statistical leverage in the 90 %-variance principal subspace (randomized CUR). The same seeds for every sampler, space and element; 18 retentions (1–9, 10–90 %).

Aggregation. Atom-centred descriptors are aggregated to one vector per frame by the per-atom mean, i.e. the descriptor's linear design row divided by N, which is what a linear or average-kernel model (SNAP, MTP, ChIMES, GAP) actually sees. The ChIMES A-matrix space is a per-atom mean by construction; the ChIMES fingerprint and the Euler characteristic curve (chi(r)/N of the periodic Rips filtration, 0–6 Å) are whole-frame descriptors. The earlier mean + std concatenation adds a descriptor-independent heterogeneity axis (large for any inhomogeneous cell) that inflates surface enrichment by about 25 %; it is kept in the repository as a sensitivity variant (export tags without _mean) but not shown here.

Hyperparameters. Each element uses the settings of the expert-optimised GAP / NNP / SNAP / MTP models of Zuo et al. (per-element cutoffs; MTP levels 16–24) and T. Sundberg's ChIMES fits for the A-matrix and fingerprint spaces. The Behler η values are the paper's Å⁻² numbers (n2p2 Bohr⁻² inputs converted). Li's ChIMES fit has no 4-body term, so its fingerprint is 2b + 3b only, and its 22 smallest cells have too few 3-body clusters and are excluded from that one space.
